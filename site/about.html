About Kintsugi

Small company.
Local by design.

What we believe

The best tools get out of the way.

A small set of rules keeps every decision simple — and every tool predictable.

Local-first, always

Your data is yours. No upload, no account, no API key. Tools work offline because your machine is the product, not a client for someone else's service.

Determinism over drama

Safety and correctness decisions come from rules, parsers, and proofs. Models can help explain, but they never get the final vote on whether a command is safe or a result is valid.

Small enough to audit

We keep the codebase focused and the licenses permissive — MIT OR Apache-2.0. You should be able to read, fork, and trust what you run.

No telemetry, no surprises

Nothing phones home. No usage metrics, no crash pings, no silent updates that change the deal. If a tool needs network access, you'll hear about it.

The name

A quiet accent, and a standard.

Kintsugi takes its name from a craft where the join is finished in gold rather than hidden. We kept the part that matters to us: a single thin seam, used sparingly, as a mark of care in an otherwise quiet interface. It is the only decoration in anything we ship.
